How I Approach Enablement

Understand

Clarify the objective, user needs, process and expected outcome before supporting implementation.

Explain

Break procedures into clear, practical steps appropriate to the people using them.

Practise

Encourage hands-on application, questions and clarification rather than relying only on one-way instruction.

Follow Up

Check implementation, identify gaps, provide feedback and track outstanding actions.

Measure

Use records, reports and available data to understand progress and identify areas requiring attention.

Improve

Capture feedback and lessons learned and use them to strengthen processes and future support.
